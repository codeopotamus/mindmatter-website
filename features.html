Wellbeing check-ins

Eighteen real screeners, not quizzes someone made up.

Every questionnaire in Mind Matter is a published, validated instrument used in actual clinical practice — cited to its original paper, scored the way the research defines, and tracked over time so you can see a direction rather than a single number.

A score is a signal, not a verdict. Screening instruments are designed to flag "this is worth a proper look" — they cannot diagnose, and they can be wrong in both directions. Only a licensed clinician can diagnose a condition.

When it gets bad

Built for your worst night, not just your average one.

Most wellness apps are designed for the version of you that's already doing okay. The hardest moments need something different — fewer words, bigger buttons, and a plan you wrote before you needed it.

  • SOS Calm — guided box breathing and grounding, no reading required
  • Crisis plan — your warning signs, your people, what helps, written in advance
  • Local helplines — the right crisis number for the country you're in
  • DBT distress tolerance — skills for riding out the peak without making it worse
To be completely clear: Mind Matter cannot monitor you and cannot respond to an emergency. In a crisis, call or text 988 (US), text HOME to 741741, or call 911. An app is not a substitute for a person.
