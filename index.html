What Mind Matter is

A private place to notice how you're actually doing.

Mind Matter is a personal mental wellness app for iPhone, made by Mind Matter LLC. It gives you a single, private place to log how you feel, write freely, complete well-known wellbeing questionnaires, follow self-guided therapy-informed programs, and see the patterns that emerge over weeks and months.

Most people carry their mental health around in fragments — a mood you forgot by Thursday, a bad week you can't quite explain, a thing that helped once but you can't remember what it was. Mind Matter exists to hold those fragments together and hand them back to you as something you can actually read.

It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, or cure anything. There is no clinician on the other end. It is a tool for self-awareness and healthy habits, designed to sit alongside professional care — never to replace it.

A look inside

Designed to be calm at 2am.

Reflection, not diagnosis

Someone to think out loud with.

Bandura is Mind Matter's AI companion. It comes into a session already knowing what you've logged recently, so you don't have to start from scratch every time — and it can pick up a thread from weeks ago that you'd forgotten.

  • Jump straight in — “I'm anxious”, “I need to vent”, “help me sleep”
  • Notices patterns across sessions and surfaces them back to you
  • Talk or type, whichever you have the energy for
  • Available at 3am, which is usually when you need it
Being straight with you: Bandura is an AI character — not a person, not a licensed professional, and not supervised by one. AI can be confidently wrong. Treat what it says as a prompt for your own thinking, never as clinical advice.

Privacy

This app holds the most personal thing you own.

We built it accordingly. The short version: your content is yours, we don't sell it, we don't run ads, and you can take it or destroy it at any moment.

Never sold

Delete for real

If you're in crisis, please don't use an app

Mind Matter cannot monitor you and cannot respond to emergencies. If you're thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or you're in immediate danger, reach a person now:

  • 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text, US)
  • Text HOME to 741741 — Crisis Text Line
  • 911 — or your local emergency number, if there's immediate danger to life
  • Outside the US — find your local line at findahelpline.com
