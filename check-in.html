What this is and isn't. A screening indicator — a signal that something may be worth a proper look by a professional. It is not a diagnosis, it does not account for your history or circumstances, and a low score does not mean nothing is wrong. If any of this does not match your lived experience, trust yourself and talk to someone qualified.

One score is a dot. Ten is a line.

A single result tells you about today. Tracking the same screener over weeks tells you whether things are actually moving — which is the part that matters, and the part Mind Matter is built to do.

About these instruments

Why only these three?

The app ships eighteen validated screeners. Only three appear on this public page — the ones whose licences unambiguously permit free commercial use. The rest are free for clinical and research use but have not been cleared for a commercial website, so they stay in the app rather than being reproduced here.

🧠

PHQ-9 · Mood

Nine items on depressive symptoms over two weeks. Public domain. Kroenke, Spitzer & Williams, J Gen Intern Med, 2001.

💭

GAD-7 · Anxiety

Seven items on generalised anxiety over two weeks. Public domain. Spitzer, Kroenke, Williams & Löwe, Arch Intern Med, 2006.

⚡

ASRS · Focus

The WHO's six-question adult ADHD screener, scored with its real per-item thresholds rather than a naive sum. Kessler et al., 2005.
